    Two Open Postdoc Positions¶

    We are seeking outstanding and highly motivated postdoctoral candidates to join the School of Informatics at University of Tsukuba, Japan.

    Required Qualifications: . PhD (or close to completion of PhD) in Computer Science, Information Systems or a related area . Applications with research experience in data mining, text mining, artificial intelligence, or information retrieval are welcome . Strong publication record with publications in top level conferences and/or journals . Strong research skills . Strong programming skills . Strong verbal and written communication skills . Self-motivated with a strong work ethic and ability to work independently

    The postdoctoral researcher will be working under the supervision of Dr. Haitao Yu as well as in collaboration with project collaborators: Professor Adam Jatowt. A particular advantage of the position is that it is a pure research position, with no need for administrative tasks like deliverable writing etc. The position is initially for one year, with the possibility of extension for additional 1-2 years upon mutual consent. Starting time should be from November/December, 2020 but this can be negotiable.

    Applicants are encouraged to assemble and submit the application package composed of CV, a 1-2 page summary of the past research accomplishments, 1-2 representative publications and contact information for 1-2 reference providers. Application package should be sent by e-mail to: yuhaitao@slis.tsukuba.ac.jp

    All applications arriving before September 21, 2020, will be given full consideration, but the call remains open until the position has been filled.
