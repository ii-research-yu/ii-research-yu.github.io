Hai-Tao Yu received the Ph.D. degree in 2013 from the Faculty of Engineering, <a href="http://www.tokushima-u.ac.jp/english/">University of Tokushima</a>, Japan. From 2014 to now, he is working at <a href="http://www.global.tsukuba.ac.jp">University of Tsukuba</a> as an assistant professor. His research focuses on Information Retrieval and Data Mining. From Feb. 2015 to Jan. 2017, he was visiting at the <a href="https://www.gla.ac.uk/schools/computing/research/researchoverview/informationretrieval/#tabs-1">IR group</a> of <a href="https://www.gla.ac.uk/schools/computing/">School of Computing Science</a>, <a href="https://www.gla.ac.uk">University of Glasgow</a> sponsored by University of Tsukuba. He is a member of IEEE and ACM. He is a member of the organizers of the NTCIR tasks: <a href="http://ntcirtemporalia.github.io/">Temporalia-2</a> and <a href="http://ntcirakg.github.io/index.html">AKG</a>.
